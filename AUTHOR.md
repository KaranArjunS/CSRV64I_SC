## Authorship and Intent

CSRV64I_SC is a **single-author project**.

All architecture design, RTL implementation, verification setup, and documentation
have been created and maintained by **Karan Arjun S**.

This repository is shared publicly with the intent of:

- Helping students understand CPU architecture through readable RTL
- Serving as a reference implementation for RV64I
- Supporting independent research and experimentation

There are currently no external contributors.
Contributions may be considered in the future, but architectural direction
remains intentionally cohesive and author-driven.
